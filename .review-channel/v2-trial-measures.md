**Lessons from flow 2 (lead, for flow 3 and the template):**
1. Put the action × state table and the data shapes in the Architect plan before the contract; the App planner then found 78 fixture cells that should have been there from G1.
2. Architect plans the App in outline only; the App planner (G1b) does the detail after the BE is final.
3. Every answer example is an exact real answer (FC-41); shape-only examples hid two bugs.
4. Money formulas get worked examples with numbers in the plan (my FC-21 formula double-counted; the fixer caught it).
5. Fresh scoped reviewers/re-checks were cheaper and sharper than resumed ones.
6. Settle G3 fix directions (with Fable for money) before the improver starts.
7. Flow-lead field names are checked against the existing contract before they go to agents (FC-47 lineKind slip).
8. Tester steps must state the seeded rules (approval limits, REQUIRED dimension rules, role permissions); 3 of 5 sim HIGH candidates were wrong expectations.
9. One-use session files slow the tester; plan sessions per step up front. Re-test data gets used up: seed fresh cases for re-tests.
10. Simulator money tests found the real bugs (4 HIGH) that two review rounds missed: keep the stack run before approval, and always re-test a money fix on the stack.
