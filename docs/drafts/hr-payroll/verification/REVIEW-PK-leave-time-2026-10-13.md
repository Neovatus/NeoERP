# Review: PK leave and time verification (2026-10-13)

Independent re-check of `PK-leave-time-verification.md`, covering its DIFFERS rows and the MATCH claims for annual leave of 14 days, 9 h a day / 48 h a week and double-rate overtime. Every source was downloaded again and its text extracted from the PDF. Nothing else was edited.

Notes before the table:
- The input file says there are 17 DIFFERS rows, but its tables contain only 16 (the IDs listed below). Its totals line (18/17/14) is off by one.
- `check_state` in h2 §4.3 only allows `UNCHECKED` or `CHECKED`. The labels VERIFIED-OFFICIAL and VERIFIED-SECONDARY used below are not in the contract. Either add them to the enum, or ship `CHECKED` and put the class in `evidence_version` or `notes`.
- **New source the input missed.** Balochistan has its own Factories Act 2021 (Act XIX of 2021) and Shops and Establishments Act 2021 (Act XVII of 2021). Both are official Provincial Assembly texts:
  - BA-FA: https://pabalochistan.gov.pk/storage/4216/2021-07-16_11_03_05_66551.pdf
  - BA-SH: https://pabalochistan.gov.pk/storage/4219/2021-07-16_11_04_05_d24d7.pdf
  - Amendment: https://pabalochistan.gov.pk/storage/4264/2022-06-14_08_41_17_d121e.pdf
- **Pre-2022 Factories Act 1934 text used here as the federal (ICT) wording.** It comes from a secondary copy of the Punjab-amended text, whose footnotes quote the omitted s.54 and the earlier s.50 wording: https://khalidzafar.com/wp-content/files_mf/1759507924TheFactoriesAct1934.pdf
