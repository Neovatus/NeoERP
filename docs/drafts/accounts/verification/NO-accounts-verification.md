# Norway Accounts pack verification (NO@2026.2 / TAX-NO@2026.2)

Checked by: research accountant for the Accounts lead, 2026-10-10. This is the Accounts lead's verification (Jabran's rule R-9: no external auditor; this state = `CHECKED`).
Method: official sources fetched with WebFetch on 2026-10-10 (Lovdata, Skatteetaten, the Skatteetaten SAF-T GitHub repository that publishes the Regnskap Norge standard-account list and the standard tax-code list). Date per row = the page's "sist endret" marker or the file's last commit date. WebSearch was used only to find pages, never as a source.

**Fetch limits (said, not guessed):**
- **NS 4102 itself could not be read.** NS 4102 is a Standard Norge publication and is paid. The only official, publisher-issued chart text I could read is the **SAF-T "General Ledger Standard Accounts, 4 character"** list (copyright Regnskap Norge AS; published in Skatteetaten's repository https://github.com/Skatteetaten/saf-t, folder `General Ledger Standard Accounts/CSV`, file `General_Ledger_Standard_Accounts_4_character.csv`, last commit 2023-02-01 "Small changes in general ledger standard accounts 4 characters"; 740 accounts; the 2020 file differs by only a few names). Every chart row below is checked against that list. It is the reference Skatteetaten uses for SAF-T mapping, so it is the right check for a chart that fills `std_account_ref`; it does not prove an account is in NS 4102. The licence note says it may be used only "in connection with SAF-T mapping"; other commercial use must be agreed with Regnskap Norge AS (https://www.regnskapnorge.no). **The Accounts lead must settle that licence point before the NO chart ships any of those numbers or names** (our own numbering and English names are our own; the Norwegian names in `name_local` are Regnskap Norge's wording on many rows).
- **Standard tax codes:** read from `Standard Tax Codes/CSV/Standard_Tax_Codes.csv` in the same repository (last commit 2021-04-14). The older Skatteetaten PDF "Norwegian SAF-T Standard VAT/Tax codes" (V1.13, 2018-03-23) was read by text extraction only for the code notes. It predates the 2022 mva-melding.
- **The mva-melding line numbers** (the numbered lines of the current return) were **not** read. The pack maps by SAF-T standard code, and those codes are CHECKED. The line numbering stays UNCLEAR.
- **Stortinget's 2026 VAT decision** (the document that sets 25 / 15 / 12 %) could not be read: the PDF at stortinget.no is compressed and merverdiavgiftsloven's rate sections were outside the text WebFetch returned. Rates are CHECKED on Skatteetaten's rates page instead.
- merverdiavgiftsloven (Lovdata, "sist endret" LOV-2025-12-22-121 from 2026-07-01): the page returned only part of the law. § 11-1 second and third paragraph was read; § 15-x terms, § 4-7 detail, § 3-30 and § 11-3 text were not read in full.
- bokføringsforskriften (FOR-2004-12-01-1558, "sist endret" FOR-2026-09-29-1933): §§ 5-1-1 to 5-1-6 read. §§ 5-3 / 5-4 (cash sales) were not in the text returned.
- A-melding pages and the arbeidsgiveravgift timing rule for feriepenger: Skatteetaten's 2026 notice and the A-melding holiday page do not state it. See row 4.14.

## Summary

### Must change (publish as 2026.3 with `CHECKED`)

1. **`HOLIDAY_PAY_EMPLOYER_CONTRIB_ACCRUED` (packs §6.2a, §6.3, payroll-v2-p2 lines 294–295): NO account = 2781, not 2785.** In the standard list **2785 is "Påløpt finansskatt andre honorarer"** (accrued finansskatt on other remuneration). **2781 = "Påløpt arbeidsgiveravgift ferielønn"** (accrued employer's contribution on holiday pay). Proposed row: `2781`, name_local *Påløpt arbeidsgiveravgift ferielønn*, name_en *Accrued employer's contribution on holiday pay*, LIABILITY, credit, control PAYROLL, `source` "Regnskap Norge SAF-T standard account 2781 (Skatteetaten repository, 4-character list, commit 2023-02-01)". The expense side: the standard account is **5405** *Arbeidsgiveravgift av påløpte feriepenger*. Our chart has **5410** with that name; **5410 is not in the standard list**. Use 5405 (keep `EMPLOYER_CONTRIB_EXPENSE` = 5400 as the role, and let the payroll journal post this accrual's expense to 5405 only if the lead wants it separate; no new role, D-85/D-97). The chart row 2780 is named "…på opptjente feriepenger", but **2780 = *Påløpt arbeidsgiveravgift påløpt lønn*** (accrued on accrued *wages*): rename it to the standard name and leave it without a role. Correct the `source` text of 2780, 5410.
2. **`EMPLOYEE_ADVANCES` (packs §6.2a): NO account = 1572, not 1570.** In the standard list **1570 = "Reiseforskudd"** (travel advances) and **1572 = "Lønnsforskudd (som trekkes i lønn senere)"** (salary advances recovered from pay). Proposed row: `1572`, name_local *Lønnsforskudd (som trekkes i lønn senere)*, name_en *Salary advances to employees (recovered from pay)*, ASSET, debit, control PAYROLL, `source` "Regnskap Norge SAF-T standard account 1572". Loans to employees that are not salary advances = **1573** *Andre kortsiktige lån til ansatte (ikke forskudd lønn)* (no role). The chart's own **1575 "Forskudd til ansatte" must go**: in the standard list 1575 = *Mellomregning aksjonær/deltaker* (shareholder current account). The chart's 1570 "Andre kortsiktige fordringer" collides with *Reiseforskudd*; move it to **1579** *Andre kortsiktige fordringer*.
3. **`HOLIDAY_PAY_EXPENSE` = 5020 is not a standard account.** The standard account is **5092** *Feriepenger*. Remap the role to 5092 (name_local *Feriepenger*), or keep 5020 as a flagged NeoERP extension. The chart's `source` text "SAF-T standard account 5020" is wrong either way. (Same wrong-claim pattern on 27 other rows, see §5 row 5.2.)
4. **VAT accounts that share a standard number but mean something else** (`SALES_TAX_OUTPUT_RC`, `SALES_TAX_INPUT_RC`, the low-rate accounts). Standard: 2700 høy / 2701 middels / **2702 middels, råfisk** / **2703 lav sats** / **2704 utgående mva kjøp tjenester fra utlandet, høy** / 2705 innførsel varer høy / 2706 innførsel varer middels / 2707 innenlands omvendt avgiftsplikt høy / 2709 kjøp tjenester utlandet lav; inputs 2710 / 2711 / **2712 råfisk** / **2713 lav** / **2714 kjøp tjenester fra utlandet høy** / 2715 / 2716 / 2717 / 2718. Our chart: 2702 = low (should be **2703**), 2703 = reverse-charge output (should be **2704**), 2712 = low (should be **2713**), 2714 = RC input (matches only for services at the high rate). Proposed: low-rate output **2703**, low-rate input **2713**, `SALES_TAX_OUTPUT_RC` **2704**, `SALES_TAX_INPUT_RC` **2714**. `tax-codes.csv` rows move with them (NO-MVA-12 output 2702 → 2703, input 2712 → 2713; NO-MVA-RC-SVC output 2703 → 2704). Import VAT (2705/2715, 2706/2716), domestic reverse charge (2707/2717) and RC-services-at-12 % (2709/2718) become the accounts the named overrides of NO-1 point to (the NO-1 codes have none today).

   - `NO-MVA-IMPORT-0` is shown as **84**: **84 = import of goods, 15 % (middle rate), *without* deduction**. Zero-rate import is **85**; `NO-MVA-IMPORT-EXEMPT` also carries 85, so the two are duplicates. Drop `NO-MVA-IMPORT-0` or add `NO-MVA-IMPORT-15-NODED` for 84.

   - Add the exact invoice text for the seller of `NO-MVA-RC-DOM-SALE` (51): **"Omvendt avgiftsplikt – Merverdiavgift ikke beregnet"** (bokføringsforskriften § 5-1-1 point 7). The buyer org.nr is then always required (§ 5-1-2).

   - **3020 / 3030 / 3040** (ours: medium, low, services; standard: 3020 *tjenester, høy sats*, 3030 *handelsvarer, middels sats*, 3040 *egentilvirkede varer, middels sats*, **3050 *tjenester, lav sats***). Proposed: `SERVICE_REVENUE_DEFAULT` = **3020**, medium-rate sales **3030**, low-rate sales **3050**, drop our 3040.
   - **3080** `DISCOUNT_GIVEN` (not standard; standard discount accounts are 3081–3089, per rate); **3090** `SALES_RETURNS` (standard 3090 = *Viderefakturering av omkostninger ved salg, høy sats*): use a free number with the extension flag, or **3088** *Annen salgsinntektsreduksjon, avgiftspliktig salg, middels sats* family for returns.
   - **3710** `ROYALTY_INCOME` (standard 3710 = *Provisjonsinntekt, unntatt avgiftsplikt*).
   - **4380** `DISCOUNT_RECEIVED` (standard 4380 = *Grunnlag MVA innførsel av varer, høy sats*, a memorandum base account): the standard purchase-discount accounts are **4371 / 4372** *Kontantrabatt, varer for videresalg, høy / middels sats* and 4375 / 4376 (quantity discount) and 4378 / 4379 (other).

   - Also rename: 2610 name_local → *Utleggstrekk* (ours *Påleggstrekk*); 2099 is *Udisponert resultat* in the standard (ours *Årets resultat*, role `CURRENT_YEAR_RESULT`; the standard annual-result account is **8800** *Årsresultat*, and *Årsresultat* closes to 2099, so the role may stay on 2099 if the English name says "Result for the year (undistributed)").
7. **Retention row `NO.RETENTION.BOOKKEEPING`:** the value **5 years** is CHECKED (bokføringsloven § 13 second paragraph; items 1–4 five years after the end of the fiscal year; items 5–8 three years and six months). ### Should change

- Rename the NO-MVA-EXEMPT (6) label (see 1.6): code 6 is "Omsetning og uttak utenfor merverdiavgiftsloven", which in the 2018 Skatteetaten code note includes sales falling outside chapter 3 (the exemption chapter). "Outside the VAT Act" misleads; use *Unntatt eller utenfor merverdiavgiftsloven (kode 6)*. Code 5 official wording: *Innenlands omsetning og uttak fritatt for merverdiavgift* (zero rate, deduction allowed).
- Add the unreported standard codes the list carries and the pack lacks: **0** (purchases, no VAT treatment), **7** (income, no VAT treatment), **84** (see Must 5), and the optional compensation-basis codes 20, 21, 22 (not mandatory per the 2018 note).
- `cash_payment_warn_limit` NO: value is right, wording is not. Skatteloven § 6-51: costs need payment through a bank or another licensed payment provider to be deductible **unless the payments total less than NOK 10,000**; several payments for one delivery, service or contract count as one. So warn at **NOK 10,000 or more**, not "above 10,000", and count the contract total.
- `PENSION_EXPENSE` = 5420 (standard 5420 = *Innberetningspliktig pensjonskostnad utbetalt over drift*, pension paid by the business directly). The employer's OTP premium is **5950** *Obligatorisk tjenestepensjon (OTP)* (or 5943 / 5944 pension insurance). Map `PENSION_EXPENSE` to 5950, or say 5420 is for directly paid pension.
- Name hygiene: 28 chart rows are not in the standard list (§5 row 5.2); they are legitimate extensions only if flagged as such; the 63-row header count (classes and groups) is not checked against NS 4102 group names (not readable).
- Packs §7.3 NO-2 "all ten payroll roles present": see §4; replace with the corrected numbers.
- `e-invoice` dates in accounts-docout §5.1: not re-read here beyond the Lovdata marker (§§ 10 and 13 amended from 2027-01-01) which agrees with N-2.
