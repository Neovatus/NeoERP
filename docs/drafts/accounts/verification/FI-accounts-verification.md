## Summary

### Must change (packs 2026.2 → publish as 2026.3 with `CHECKED`)

1. **§3.2 amending-act reference:** `FI-ALV-STD` source says "laki 1061/2024 *verify*". 1061/2024 is not the act. The 25.5 % rate is in AVL 84 §, as amended by **laki 462/2024** (in force 2024-09-01; applies when the tax liability arises on or after that date). Replace the text with "AVL 84 § (laki 462/2024, voimaan 2024-09-01)". 25.5 % itself is CHECKED.
2. **§3.2 / D-89: the FI reduced rate is one code, not two.** The whole 14 % band moved to 13.5 % on 2026-01-01 (AVL 85 § 1 mom., laki 1358/2025). The return box does not change: field **302** is named "Tax on domestic sales at 14 % or 13.5 %" (VSRALVKV v1.9, 2025-12-03). So by the pack's own rule (§3: rate version when the box stays), `FI-ALV-14` and `FI-ALV-13-5` must be **one code with two rate versions**: 14.00 to 2025-12-31 and 13.50 from 2026-01-01. Proposed: keep the code `FI-ALV-14`'s identity but rename it `FI-ALV-RED` (nameLocal "Alennettu verokanta") with `tax_code_rate_versions` (1) 14.0000 from 2025-01-01, (2) 13.5000 from 2026-01-01; delete `FI-ALV-13-5` and `FI-ALV-IN-13-5` as separate codes (keep `FI-ALV-IN-RED`). Fix the D-89 example: "FI food 14 → 13.5 %" is **not** a box change, so it is not an example of a new code. (If Jabran prefers two codes for visibility, both map to box 302 and the rule text must say so.) The 10 % to 14 % move of 2025-01-01 (books, passenger transport, and others) **was** a box change (303 → 302); that is the example D-89 should use.
3. **§3.2 FI-ALV-14 / FI-ALV-10 scope text.** "14 from 2013-01-01 (since 2025-01-01 also books, medicines, passenger transport, accommodation, culture, sport)" is right as a story but must be recorded as: **13.5 % from 2026-01-01** covers food and drink, feed, restaurant and catering (not alcohol or tobacco), passenger transport, accommodation, medicines, menstrual and incontinence products and children's diapers, books, sport and sports facilities, admission to cultural events, performers' fees (registered), art objects (79 c §, conditions), copyright-society fees, and **Yleisradio's public funding (moved from 10 % on 2026-01-01, laki 921/2024)**. **10 % from 2026-01-01** is **only newspapers and periodicals** (physical or electronic; not mainly advertising, not mainly video or music) (AVL 85 a §). Update the `FI-ALV-10` text from "newspapers, periodicals *verify*" to this, with the CHECKED source.
4. **§3.2 `FI-ALV-RC-NONEU-SVC` "verify the field":** the official answer is **no base box at all**. Services bought from a business established **outside** the EU: report the VAT in field **301/302/303** (the domestic-sales-by-rate box of the rate that applies) and, if deductible, also in **307**. The purchase amount is not reported anywhere (OmaVero instructions). So the code's return mapping is: output tax → 301 (at 25.5 %), deduction → 307, base → none. Fix the pack row and E's FI box mapping.
5. **E FI-KVI box set (TAX-11 "missing FI bases"):** replace the free-text list of §3.3 of E with the exact box list in section 2 below (fields 301–320 and 056, with the 308 formula). The bases that the pack text leaves out are: **309** (zero-rated turnover), **310** (imports, base), **311** (sales of goods to other EU countries), **312** (sales of services to other EU countries), **313** (purchases of goods from other EU countries, base), **314** (purchases of services from other EU countries, base), **319** (construction and scrap sales, value), **320** (construction and scrap purchases, base). **Domestic sales at 25.5 / 13.5 / 10 % have no base box**, only tax (301–303). Fields 315, 316, 317, 336, 337 (small-business relief and cash basis) can no longer be filed from tax period 2025-01 (specification section 2); do not seed them.
6. **E FI channel label (TAX-11):** the file is **VSRALVKV** (Tax Administration data-file specification, "Return for self-assessed taxes, VAT"). Official channels are OmaVero (manual entry), an **API**, or **upload of this file to Ilmoitin.fi** (vero.fi form description 4001, updated 2026-06-10). Label the NeoERP file "Ilmoitin.fi file (VSRALVKV)" and the manual path "OmaVero". "Field codes per Ilmoitin.fi record layout" in E §3.3 is right; the layout is the specification above.
7. **§4.5 / E C-8 `eu_sales_list_frequency_default` for FI:** the yhteenvetoilmoitus is **monthly only**, due the **20th of the following month** (AVL 162 §; VSRALVYV section 2). There is **no quarterly option** and no frequency setting to offer. Remove "FI: MONTHLY or QUARTERLY" from E C-8 and §4.1. A company with no EU sales in a month files nothing. Set `eu_sales_list_services_frequency_default` to MONTHLY for FI (same list, same date).
8. **§6.2 / §6.3 payroll names ("sotu"):** the employer's contribution is the **työnantajan sairausvakuutusmaksu** (laki 771/2016), **1.91 % in 2026** (1.87 % in 2025), paid to Verohallinto together with the withheld tax. "Sotu" (sosiaaliturvamaksu) is the name from the older act that law 771/2016 repealed. Rename: 2770 "**Työnantajan sairausvakuutusmaksuvelka**" (was "Sairausvakuutusmaksuvelka"), 5400 "**Työnantajan sairausvakuutus- ja työttömyysvakuutusmaksut**" (was "Sosiaaliturva- ja työttömyysvakuutusmaksut"); keep "sotu" only as a search alias in the role help text. Payroll P2 rows that say "sotu" should say "sairausvakuutusmaksu".
9. **New account, role `HOLIDAY_PAY_EMPLOYER_CONTRIB_ACCRUED` (FI, required):** **FI 2941 "Lomapalkkavelan henkilösivukulujaksotus"** (English: "Accrued employer contributions on holiday pay"), LIABILITY, credit, control PAYROLL, `payee_tag` OPTIONAL, balance-sheet line *Siirtovelat* (D. Vieras pääoma). The pack §2.3 already lists 2941 with a slightly different name ("Lomapalkkojen henkilösivukulujaksotus") but gives it no role; give it this role and this name. Source: kirjanpitolaki 4 luku 6 § (*siirtovelkoja ovat* costs that belong to the period on an accrual basis but are not yet paid; Finlex, read 2026-10-10) and the balance-sheet line *Siirtovelat* in kirjanpitoasetus 1 luku 6 §. No official chart gives the number; it is NeoERP's (see row 5.10). Dr 5400 / Cr 2941 (one line per component, each with its own payee: Verohallinto for the sairausvakuutusmaksu, the pension insurer for TyEL, the Employment Fund for unemployment insurance); released when holiday pay is paid, Dr 2941 / Cr 2770, 2771, 2772.
10. **New account, role `EMPLOYEE_ADVANCES` (FI, required):** **FI 1570 "Työntekijöiden ennakot ja lainasaamiset"** (English "Advances and loans to employees"), ASSET, debit, control PAYROLL, `payee_tag` OPTIONAL, balance-sheet line *II Saamiset – Muut saamiset* (or *Lainasaamiset* for a formal loan) within current assets. 1570 is free in the FI chart (group 15 has 1500, 1550, 1580 in §2.3) and is the same number as NO 1570 and the IS proposal. A loan due after more than one year is a long-term receivable by kirjanpitolaki 4 luku 7 § (*pitkäaikainen saaminen*); the role stays current, and the lead can add a long-term sibling later. No law prescribes the account; the statement lines are kirjanpitoasetus 1 luku 6 §.
11. **§4.1a / attachments §3 retention wording (state it as two classes):** CHECKED value, but add the rule that **ledgers, chart of accounts, financial statements, annual report and the list of ledgers and material are ten years from the end of the financial year, vouchers and the rest six years from the end of the calendar year in which the financial year ended** (kirjanpitolaki 2 luku 10 §, see row 4.1). Also add **payroll books (palkkakirjanpito): ten years from the end of the financial year** (OVML 28 §). In D-143 ("one period per country") the voucher period is therefore too short for anything that is a *kirjanpito* (ledger, journal, payroll register) or a statement; make those classes 10 years for FI, or mark in D-143 that FI ledgers and statements are exported and kept as `LEDGER_EXPORT` at 10 years.

### Should change

- **`FI-ALV-RC-EU-GOODS` and `FI-ALV-IMPORT` reduced-rate versions.** AVL 85 § applies the 13.5 % rate also to intra-EU acquisitions and imports (*yhteisöhankinnasta … maahantuonnista*), and the return sums the tax of all rates into one field (305 and 304). A food importer needs the 13.5 and 10 % variants (`FI-ALV-RC-EU-GOODS-RED`, `FI-ALV-IMPORT-RED`, `FI-ALV-IMPORT-10`). Today the pack lists only the 13.5 % import code "for the auditor, not seeded". Seed them, or make the rate selectable on these two codes.
- **`FI-ALV-IN-STD-NODED`:** AVL 114 § denies the deduction for more than representation: housing or leisure property and its related supplies, commuting transport, **passenger cars, motorcycles, caravans, pleasure boats, and aircraft up to 1,550 kg**, with their running costs. Add a second non-deductible code (`FI-ALV-IN-STD-NODED-CAR` or a general reason) or document that the person marks the line non-deductible.
- **Returnable packaging (AVL 85 b §):** deposit-bearing packaging and transport supplies are always 25.5 %, even when the contents are at 13.5 %. Add one line to the FI tax-code help text.
- **Invoice-issue deadline (AVL 209 c §):** an invoice for an intra-EU supply of goods must be issued by the **15th day of the month after the delivery month**; the same 15th-day rule applies to services under the general rule (vero.fi invoice guidance). Add a WARN to B for FI-ALV-EU-GOODS / FI-ALV-EU-SVC documents.
- **Return with no activity:** a VAT-registered taxpayer files for **every** period; with no VAT activity, the file carries **056 = 1** (no activity), and 056 and 301–320 cannot be mixed. It can be filed at most six periods ahead (monthly) or two (quarterly). Add a "No activity" action to E's FI return.
- **Corrections (D-93):** CORRECT_EARLIER is right (a replacement return for the same period, OVML 25 §, within three years). The specification also allows a **financially insignificant** error to be corrected on a later period without a replacement return. Offer NEXT_RETURN as an explicit choice for small amounts, default CORRECT_EARLIER.
- **P&L layout (FI):** the official statement (kirjanpitoasetus 1 luku 1 §) puts the subtotal **"Voitto (tappio) ennen tilinpäätössiirtoja ja veroja"** before *Tilinpäätössiirrot*, then *Tuloverot* and *Muut välittömät verot*, then *Tilikauden voitto (tappio)*. The pack's order "RESULT_AFTER_FINANCIAL_ITEMS → APPROPRIATIONS → RESULT_BEFORE_TAX → INCOME_TAX" shows the subtotal after the appropriations and calls it "before tax". For FI label the subtotal "Voitto (tappio) ennen tilinpäätössiirtoja ja veroja" and put it before appropriations; add a separate line for *Muut välittömät verot* (other direct taxes). The balance sheet line for 2085 is officially **"Verotusperäiset varaukset"** (kirjanpitoasetus 1 luku 6 §: B. Tilinpäätössiirtojen kertymä: *Poistoero*, *Verotusperäiset varaukset*); rename 2085 "Vapaaehtoiset varaukset" to "Verotusperäiset varaukset (vapaaehtoiset varaukset)".
- **`FI.RETENTION.BOOKKEEPING`:** change `check_state` to CHECKED and replace the single key by two keys: `FI.RETENTION.LEDGERS` 10 years from FY end, `FI.RETENTION.VOUCHERS` 6 years from the end of the calendar year of the FY end (row 4.1).
- **VAT on property (adjustment period) and other longer retention:** kirjanpitolaki 2 luku 10 § 2 mom. says "unless another law sets a longer period". AVL has such rules for property investments; not read. Keep a note in the help text.
- **Call-off stock report:** AVL 162 § 4 item and form 9634 (VSRALVCO) cover call-off stock arrangements. Not in the pack; note as out of scope for 2026.2.
