## 3. Open points: answers and recommendations

| # | Answer | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| OP-1 `#TAXAR` | SIE 4C (R-5): "#TAXAR — Taxeringsår som SRU-koderna avser"; it names *which year's declaration form* the SRU codes belong to; format `ÅÅÅÅ`; optional. "Taxeringsår" left Swedish law with SFL (2011:1244) from 2013, but its fixed meaning is inkomstår + 1, and the inkomstår of a juridisk person is the calendar year in which its beskattningsår (= räkenskapsår) ends. Rule: **`#TAXAR` = calendar year of `#RAR 0` end date + 1** (FY 2026-01-01..12-31 → 2027; FY 2026-05-01..2027-04-30 → 2028). | Keep `RAR0_END_YEAR_PLUS_1`, CHECKED on lead confirmation.
