## Summary

### Must change (publish as 2026.3 with `CHECKED`)

1. **Packs §4.2, IS row (invoice-content source) and the generated `invoice_content_rules`.** The pack names only "reglugerð nr. 50/1993". The content rule is in **lög 50/1988, 20. gr.**, and in reglugerð 50/1993 arts. 3 to 5, 7 and 8. Proposed text for the IS row: *"lög um virðisaukaskatt nr. 50/1988, 20. gr.; reglugerð nr. 50/1993, 3.–5., 7. og 8. gr."* REQUIRED sales fields (20. gr., confirmed by the reglugerð art. 4):
   - issue date (*útgáfudagur*)
   - name and kennitala of buyer and of seller
   - seller's VSK registration number (*skráningarnúmer*)
   - type of sale (description, specific enough to show whether a business buyer's purchase relates to its taxable activity)
   - quantity, unit price and total price
   - the VSK amount (the reglugerð also wants it stated whether VSK is included)
   
   Also REQUIRED: a continuous number series; taxable and exempt sales shown apart, and taxable sales split by rate (art. 5); and a prepayment receipt numbered separately from invoices, which the final invoice must refer to (art. 7).
2. **Packs §4.2, IS "Simplified invoice" column (now "—").** Change it. Reglugerð 50/1993 art. 4 lets a retailer leave out the buyer's name and kennitala on cash sales to end consumers. Art. 8 lets a cash-register slip stand in for the invoice, and buyer name and kennitala may be left out on invoices of ISK 3,000 or less. Proposed text: *"reglugerð 50/1993, 4. gr. and 8. gr.: no buyer name or kennitala for consumer cash sales; ≤ ISK 3,000 for the cash-register route (read of a possibly outdated text, verify the current figure)."*
3. **§3.3 `IS-VSK-RC-SVC` (source "35. gr., imported services") and the return mapping.** The code is not "24 % on all imported services". Art. 35 says:
   - (1) a person doing VSK-exempt business pays VSK on certain services bought from a foreign business;
   - (2) a VSK-registered person pays to the extent the VSK is not deductible, and always when the service relates to importing goods;
   - (3) any other buyer pays on listed services when the purchase is **ISK 10,000 or more (excluding VSK) in a general tax period**;
   - (4) no duty when the foreign seller (or its agent or representative) is itself on the VSK register;
   - (6) the buyer reports in a **separate report** (*sérstök skýrsla*) due the 5th day of the second month after the period.
   
   Skatturinn's search result names the form as **RSK 10.24** (*Skýrsla um virðisaukaskattsskyld kaup þjónustu frá útlöndum*). The IS-VSK box set therefore needs a second report or a clear note that the RC lines go to RSK 10.24. The pack also needs the threshold (a company-level setting, ISK 10,000) and the "foreign seller registered in Iceland" exclusion. The list of services in art. 35(3) is in the unread part of the text, so the scope stays *(verify)*.
4. **§3.3 `IS-VSK-0` and `IS-VSK-EXPORT` label "undanþegin velta með rétti til innskatts".** In the law, *undanþegin* is the word of 2. gr. 3. mgr. (exempt, **no** input deduction, 4. mgr.). Sales abroad are in **12. gr.**: "Til skattskyldrar veltu telst ekki: 1. … sala … úr landi". Rename the box text to *"velta skv. 12. gr. (utan skattskyldrar veltu)"* and keep `kind ZERO`. Whether input VSK is deductible on 12. gr. sales rests on art. 16, which I read only in part. Confirm before 2026.3 (see IS-6).
5. **Payroll roles, both required by the brief, in `TAX-IS` / the IS chart:**
   - **`HOLIDAY_PAY_EMPLOYER_CONTRIB_ACCRUED` (optional): add IS 2941 "Áfallin launatengd gjöld af orlofi" (Accrued payroll costs on holiday pay).** Source: lög 113/1990 7. gr. 1. mgr. puts holiday pay (*orlofsfé og greiðslur fyrir ónotað orlof*) in the tryggingagjald base, so the employer owes tryggingagjald (6.35 % for 2026) when holiday pay is accrued. The same number as FI 2941 keeps the pack's "same number, same meaning" rule (§2.1). The IS chart has no other use of 2941 in the pack. Dr 5400 / Cr 2941; released against 2770 when the holiday pay is paid. Pension fund contribution (*mótframlag*) on holiday pay also accrues, but the rule is UNCLEAR (IS-29), so do not hard-code a percentage.
   - **`EMPLOYEE_ADVANCES` (required for IS per the brief): add IS 1570 "Fyrirframgreidd laun og lán til starfsmanna" (Salary advances and loans to employees), same number as NO 1570.** Group 15 is Receivables in the IS chart (§2.2), and the table in §2.3 uses 1550 and 1580 but not 1570, so 1570 is free there. Check the generated `is.csv` for a clash before publishing. There is no Icelandic official chart to cite (IS-34); the account is NeoERP's own proposal, and the law only requires the account structure to keep balance-sheet, expense and income accounts apart (lög 145/1994 11. gr.).
6. **Packs §4.2 / `accounts-docout.md` §5 IS cell: "Reg. 252/2018".** I found no regulation 252/2018 on e-invoicing. The Icelandic public-sector e-invoice regulation is **reglugerð nr. 44/2019** (island.is; EN 16931 under Directive 2014/55/EU). General rules on electronic invoices and bookkeeping are in **reglugerð nr. 505/2013** (in force 1 June 2013; island.is). Replace the cell with *"reglugerð 44/2019 (B2G receive, EN 16931 via TS-236)"*.
7. **IS retention row source.** Keep **7 years from the end of the fiscal year** (`FY_END`) and mark it `CHECKED`. Extend the source to: lög 145/1994 20. gr.; lög 50/1988 17. gr. 2. mgr.; reglugerð 505/2013 6. gr. (electronic records). Record the conflict (IS-19): reglugerð 50/1993 32. gr. as read says 6 years. The two laws and the newer regulation say 7, and a regulation cannot cut a statutory period. Use 7.

### Should change

- **Company currency.** Lög 145/1994 10. gr. A: books are in ISK, and certain companies may get **permission** to keep books and annual accounts in a foreign currency (conditions in another law, not read). Add a company setting `book_currency` allowed to differ from ISK for IS only after that permission; until then the IS pack must refuse a non-ISK functional currency. ISK scale 0 stays UNCLEAR (IS-20).
- **IS e-invoice B2B status** (docout §5): the EU eInvoicing page contradicts itself on a B2B mandate (summary table "no", legislation text "yes"). Keep "voluntary" and mark UNCLEAR.
- **Return frequency options** (accounts-e C-8): add the real options. Default two-month (1 + 5 days after the period); one-month by approval; special monthly returns (due 15 days after period end); annual return for businesses with turnover under ISK 4,000,000 on request by 15 February (due 5 February after the year). Agriculture is half-yearly (1 Sept / 1 March). The pack's "BIMONTHLY" default is right.
- **Tryggingagjald rate versioning.** If the pack ever carries the rate: 2026 total **6.35 %** = 4.90 % general + 1.35 % atvinnutryggingagjald + 0.05 % Ábyrgðasjóður + 0.05 % markaðsgjald (+ 0.65 % for fishing-vessel crews). Lög 113/1990 2. gr. gives the first two; skatturinn gives the total. Act 62/2026 changed the law from 1 July 2026 (some parts 1 September 2026 and 1 January 2027), so re-read before hard-coding.
