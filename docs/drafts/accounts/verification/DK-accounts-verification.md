## Summary

### Must change (publish as 2026.3 with `CHECKED`)

1. **`std_account_ref` values (packs §2.1 rule 3 "verify every reference against the published file").** The DK standardkontoplan (valid from 2026-01-01) has its own numbering: P&L 1000–3810, balance sheet 5000–8070 (603 numbers, 485 of them posting accounts; the `basis.csv` list has 449). The NeoERP numbers (shared skeleton, D-84) are therefore *not* the standard numbers, and the reference column is the only link. Use the table in §3.1 for every role account. Two of them need a decision (2090 and 2500, see 3.1).
2. **§3.1 tax codes, sources and boxes (corrections of the `Source` and `Return` columns):**
   - `DK-MOMS-RC-EU-GOODS` source: "ML § 11, **§ 46 stk. 4**" (not "§ 46 stk. 1 nr. 2"; nr. 2 is installation by a foreign business).
   - `DK-MOMS-RC-DOM` source: **ML § 46 stk. 1 nr. 4 and 6–12** (not "4–8"). Box resolved (was "verify"): the tax goes to **Salgsmoms** (output VAT) and the deduction to **Købsmoms**; no Rubrik A (momsbekendtgørelsen § 76 stk. 4, § 85). The tax must credit `SALES_TAX_OUTPUT`, not `SALES_TAX_OUTPUT_RC` (see item 3).
   - `DK-MOMS-RC-DOM-SALE` source: **ML § 46 stk. 1 nr. 4 and 6–12** (not "3–8"); stk. 2 excludes nr. 8–10 for sellers that mainly sell to private consumers. Rubrik C is right.
   - `DK-MOMS-IN-25-REP`: source "ML § 42 stk. 2 (representation, hotel/restaurant)" is wrong. Only **restaurant services** (*restaurationsydelser*) give 25 % of the VAT, and only if strictly business (ML § 42 stk. 2). Other entertainment, representation and gifts are non-deductible (stk. 1 nr. 5). Hotel stays are not in stk. 2. Rename the code's help text to "Restaurant, 25 % of VAT" and use the NODED code for the rest.
   - `DK-MOMS-0` source: "ML § 34 (e.g. newspapers)" → **ML § 34 stk. 1 nr. 18**; box Rubrik C is right (momsbekendtgørelsen § 76 stk. 11 nr. 2).
   - `DK-MOMS-EXPORT` source: "ML § 34 stk. 1" → **§ 34 stk. 1 nr. 5**. Also cover services sold to businesses outside the EU (place of supply abroad, ML §§ 14–21 d), which also go in Rubrik C (§ 76 stk. 11 nr. 1). There is no code for that today (see 2.3).
3. **Reverse-charge output account (packs §2.3 row 2705).** Output tax on EU goods and imports, and output tax on bought services, are two separate boxes on the return, and the standard chart has two accounts: **7700** *Moms af varekøb udland, EU og ikke-EU* and **7720** *Moms af ydelseskøb udland, EU og ikke-EU*. Domestic reverse-charge tax (ML § 46 stk. 1 nr. 4, 6–12) belongs on the output VAT account, **7680** *Salgsmoms*. Proposal: keep `SALES_TAX_OUTPUT_RC` on 2705 for **goods and imports** (std 7700); add **2706** *Moms af ydelseskøb i udlandet med omvendt betalingspligt* (std 7720) as a DK country role or a second RC role; post `DK-MOMS-RC-DOM` through `SALES_TAX_OUTPUT` (2700, std 7680). Momsbekendtgørelsen § 76 stk. 1 and § 76 stk. 2 allow the system to produce the nine special accounts from codes, so E can still report by code, but SAF-T mapping to 7700 / 7720 / 7680 needs the split.
4. **Simplified invoice (packs §4.2 DK row).** The source is **momsbekendtgørelsen § 66** (not "§ 61"; § 61 is forced-sale invoices). The limit **DKK 3,000** is right. Add: a business that mainly sells to private consumers must issue the simplified invoice (stk. 2); a full § 58 invoice is required for services to a private consumer from **DKK 5,000** and for Ligningsloven § 8 V supplies (stk. 5); and for EU distance sales outside OSS, § 62 supplies and new vehicles (stk. 6). The "20 per cent of the total" VAT statement is allowed (stk. 1 nr. 5).
5. **Legal texts for B C-33 (DA/EN, from BEK 1435 §§ 59, 60, 62):** reverse charge, domestic and foreign seller: **"omvendt betalingspligt, køber afregner momsen"** / "reverse charge, buyer settles the VAT" (§ 59 nr. 3, § 60); EU supply with reverse charge in the buyer's country: "omvendt betalingspligt" / "reverse charge" plus the buyer's VAT number (§ 62 stk. 1); EU exempt goods: "momsfritaget" / "0-sats" / "zero-rated" / "free of VAT" plus the buyer's VAT number (§ 62 stk. 2). Rate and tax amount may be left out in these cases (§ 59, § 60, § 62 stk. 3).
6. **Retention (attachments §3 DK row and packs §4.1a).** The value **5 years from the end of the fiscal year** is right and moves to CHECKED (Bogføringsloven § 12 stk. 1). Fix the note: § 12 is the section number *as enacted* in lov 700/2022; the note "was § 10 before lov 755/2023" is wrong (§ 10 was in the repealed 2006 act, LBK 648/2006, repealed by § 35; lov 755/2023 is a VAT-law amendment that only updated a cross-reference in skattekontrolloven § 6). Add the **10-year rule** for accounting material of a business that acquires real property or does building work used in a registered business (momsbekendtgørelsen **§ 92 stk. 3**, ML §§ 43–44), and the **1-year rule** for retail till rolls (§ 12 stk. 2; momsbekendtgørelsen § 92 stk. 1: one year from signing the annual accounts). Attachments should carry a per-document override for property documents.
7. **Whole kroner on the return.** ML § 57 stk. 1: "Beløbene angives i hele kroner, idet der bortses fra ørebeløb." E's writer for `DK-MOMS` must output whole kroner (E's DK box examples and golden files show øre). Whether "bortses fra" means truncation or rounding is not stated in the law; the Accounts lead should decide (see 3.5).
8. **EU sales list defaults (packs §4.1).** `eu_sales_list_frequency_default` MONTHLY is right. Services are **also MONTHLY by default** (BEK 1435 § 99 stk. 1); the "(verify)" on `eu_sales_list_services_frequency_default` is closed with MONTHLY. Quarterly is only by permission of Skatteforvaltningen: **goods** only if the business is not on monthly VAT periods and EU goods sales (VAT-free) are ≤ **DKK 400,000** in the current quarter and each of the previous four quarters (§ 99 stk. 4–5); **services** quarterly if not on monthly VAT periods (§ 99 stk. 6). The list is due **the 25th of the month after the period** (§ 100) in TastSelv Erhverv. Add the DKK 400,000 threshold as guidance text, like SE's SEK 500,000.
9. **Standard VAT codes (Skattestyrelsen *Momskoder – bruttoliste*, valid from 2025-12-01).** BEK 811/2026 Bilag 3 pkt. 4 (in force 2026-09-29) requires registered systems to offer **all** of Skattestyrelsen's standard VAT codes directly or to map to all of them. Add `std_vat_code_ref` (see §2.19 for the mapping of our 15 codes) and add the missing codes listed in 2.18 (at least: outside-scope S81, triangular trade S25, used goods/margin, partial-deduction rows).
10. **Layouts (packs §2.4).** `PL_DK_GROSS` source "bilag 2 skema 1" is wrong: Skema 1 is the **balance sheet** (kontoform). The P&L is **bilag 2 skema 3** (artsopdelt) or **skema 4** (funktionsopdelt); § 32 (as the pack says) lets classes B, C and D collapse **skema 3 poster 1–5** (or skema 4 poster 1–3 and 6) into *Bruttofortjeneste*. The balance sheet is **skema 1** (kontoform) or **skema 2** (kontoform, split short/long term), not "skema 3/4". "Resultat af primær drift" is not a line in skema 3; call it "Driftsresultat" (a subtotal, not a statutory line) or drop it. Source: ÅRL LBK nr 402 af 23/03/2026.
11. **EMPLOYEE_ADVANCES, DK: propose account 1570** *Forskud til medarbejdere* (English: Advances and loans to employees; `name_local` *Tilgodehavender hos medarbejdere*), ASSET / OTHER_RECEIVABLE, debit, control `PAYROLL`, `std_account_ref` **6330** *Øvrige tilgodehavender (omsætningsaktiv)*. 1570 already exists in the BASE skeleton (*Employee advances*, no role), so DK keeps the shared number. Source text for the row: "Erhvervsstyrelsen, standardkontoplan 2026-01-01, konto 6330; årsregnskabsloven bilag 2 skema 1, Omsætningsaktiver II. Tilgodehavender 6 (Andre tilgodehavender)". Do **not** use 6365 *Tilgodehavender hos direktionen*: that is for loans to directors and board (ÅRL skema 1 "Tilgodehavender hos virksomhedsdeltagere og ledelse"), and a company may have rules on those (not read).
12. **Payroll role accounts and std mapping (§4):** FERIEKONTO_PAYABLE (2772) → std **7900** *Skyldige feriepenge*; ATP_PAYABLE (2771) → **7960**; AM_CONTRIBUTION_PAYABLE (2601) → **7940**; PAYROLL_TAX_PAYABLE (2600) → **7920**; EMPLOYER_CONTRIB_PAYABLE (2770, *samlet betaling*) → **8010**; HOLIDAY_PAY_ACCRUED (2940) → **8030**. `HOLIDAY_PAY_EMPLOYER_CONTRIB_ACCRUED`: not used in DK (as decided); no DK account. `EMPLOYER_CONTRIB_EXPENSE` on 5400 maps to std 2930, but the standard puts the employer ATP expense in **2898** (inside the *Lønninger* group); see 4.5.
13. **Registration requirement dates, to be written into the pack docs (R-11, D-130):** see §1 table, rows 1.6–1.9. In short: the duty to use a digital bookkeeping system runs from the first fiscal year starting on or after **2024-07-01** (annual-report businesses using a registered system), **2025-01-01** (annual-report businesses using a non-registered system) and **2026-01-01** (other businesses with turnover above DKK 300,000 in two consecutive income years). The requirements for registered standard systems are BEK 97/2023 as rewritten by **BEK 811/2026** (in force 2026-09-29; MitID identification from **2027-03-01** and **2028-01-01**; business-document master data from **2028-01-01**). Registered systems must support **SAF-T 2.1 from 2027-01-01** and the updated standardkontoplan (valid from 2026-01-01).

### Should change

- **DK-MOMS-EU-TRIANG (new).** Skattestyrelsen code S25: triangular trade is **not** on the momsangivelse, only on the EU sales list as triangulation. The pack's `DK-EU-SALG` already lists "triangulation" with no code to feed it.
- **Late documents:** `late_document_rule` NEXT_RETURN is UNCLEAR (row 3.10). The official route for an error in a filed period is to **amend that period** in TastSelv (within 3 years); no NEXT_RETURN allowance was found. Lead decision; CORRECT_EARLIER is the safer default.
- **Tax-point date for EU goods and acquisitions:** ML §§ 24 and 25 make the tax point **the 15th of the month after delivery/acquisition, or the invoice date if earlier**. The generic `TAX_POINT` basis needs this rule in the DK pack, or EU purchase and sale tax lands in the wrong period.
- **Advance payments and bad debts, base rule:** ML § 27 stk. 1: tax on an advance is on **80 % of the amount received**; stk. 6: bad-debt relief is **80 % of the confirmed loss** (reinstated at 80 % of later receipts, in the period of payment). Document the 80 % factor (it equals VAT at 20 % of a VAT-inclusive amount).
- **Rubrik A:** the A-varer box also takes foreign businesses' installation in Denmark and distance sales into Denmark (BEK 1435 § 79 stk. 2). No code exists for these. Not needed for launch if documented.
- **Moms-API:** BEK 811/2026 Bilag 3 pkt. 7 requires registered systems to support VAT filing through Skattestyrelsen's moms-API. E's DK file is `DK_KEYING` (manual). Specification not read; plan the writer for later.
- **Standard-chart wording:** the pack says "A DK account the person adds must be mapped before it posts once H-DK ships". That is allowed (Bilag 3 pkt. 4: use the standard chart directly, or map). Note that a company is **not obliged to use every standard account** (Erhvervsstyrelsen, *Standardkontoplan*, updated 2026-09-08).
- **Cash rounding unit DKK 0.50 and FIK lengths:** UNCLEAR (rows 6.1, 6.2).
- **2945 Feriepenge, indefrosne:** UNCLEAR (row 4.9); remove or leave unmapped and mark "verify".
- **AM-bidrag note:** from 2026-01-01 AM-bidrag is not reported or paid for employees up to and including the income year they turn 17 (skat.dk). Payroll (HR lead) must know; Accounts is unaffected.

### Confirmed (CHECKED)

- **Rate:** 25 % (ML § 33). **Return periods:** half-yearly ≤ DKK 5 m, quarterly DKK 5–50 m, monthly > DKK 50 m of taxable supplies, changes only from 1 Jan / 1 Jul (ML § 57 stk. 2–8). **Due dates:** quarterly and half-yearly the 1st of the third month after (ML § 57 stk. 3–4); monthly the 25th of the next month, June by 1 month and 17 days (stk. 2).
- **Return mapping:** Rubrik A (EU acquisitions of goods; services bought from other EU countries), Rubrik B goods (listed or not listed), B services, Rubrik C; the nine special accounts; B-varer and B-ydelser must agree with the EU sales list (§ 85 stk. 4–5).
- **Invoice content:** momsbekendtgørelsen § 58 (full list in 5.1), credit notes by reference (§ 58 stk. 2), reverse-charge and EU texts (§§ 59–62).
- **Bogføringsloven:** retention 5 years (§ 12 stk. 1), digital-system duty and its dates, requirement list for registered systems.
- **Payroll:** AM-bidrag 8 %, A-skat from the eSkattekort, eIndkomst reporting, payment by the 10th (17 Jan), FerieKonto at 12.5 % for hourly-paid staff, Samlet Betaling (quarterly) and the ATP split.
