1. Receipt/payment services accept ordinary kinds today; M5 enums/tables alone do not constitute working advance/refund flows. Invoice/bill approval does not yet consume advances.
2. `AllocationCore.plan` dates `AT_POSTING` links from the source and rejects a newer target. A refund is a later target of an older credit source. Add an explicit refund-at-posting path; do not weaken ordinary invoice/payment date rules.

6. `DocumentSeries` has ADV/RFD/WO and PAY, but no SRF. Supplier-advance numbering is not explicit in C; recommend retaining PAY rather than inventing a series.
7. `fiscal_outbox.document_id` references `ar_documents`; it cannot represent a receipt directly. The proposed B-FBR advance fiscal invoice/cancellation/Annex-C extension cannot be silently implemented through that FK.

## 3. Posting entries and accounting invariants

Resolve roles on the posting date; tax accounts come from the frozen code override/role mapping, not hardcoded numbers. Every control/advance line carries CUSTOMER or SUPPLIER and the party ID; the single bank/cash line carries that party too. Branch, cost centre, voucher reference/date and base twins follow B/C/A1. In M5 currency equals company base, rate = 1 and document/base twins agree. No FX journal is produced.

Let G be gross consumed, T the stored tax share, N = G − T; for supplier input tax let D be deductible tax and U = T − D. Extraction is HALF_UP at currency scale: T = round(G × rate / (100 + rate)). Partial consumption uses the stored rate and remaining amounts; the final consumption takes the exact remaining net/tax, preventing penny residue. Partial input VAT handling is subject to Q4.

## 4. Approval, refusals and idempotency shared by every slice

| Flow | Permission and lifecycle | Approval evidence / reversal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer advance | `RECEIPT.CREATE` prepares; `RECEIPT.POST` posts. Any taxed line: DRAFT → SUBMITTED → POSTED; untaxed: ordinary one-person DRAFT → POSTED. | Taxed: DocumentApproval; untaxed: SingleStep. `RECEIPT.REVERSE`, one person, unused only. |
| Customer refund | `REFUND.CREATE` prepares/submits; `REFUND.APPROVE` approves; reject/delete/reverse under B §7.3. | Always DocumentApproval. Non-contributor including customer bank-details editors in preceding 30 days. `REFUND.REVERSE` one person. |
| Write-off | `INVOICE.WRITE_OFF` prepares; `INVOICE.APPROVE` approves under normal document lifecycle. | DocumentApproval; recovery request needs `INVOICE.WRITE_OFF` and reason; reversal needs `INVOICE.APPROVE`, caller different from requester (B §7.3). |
| Supplier advance | `PAYMENT.CREATE` prepares/submits; `PAYMENT.APPROVE` approves. Always two people, even untaxed. | DocumentApproval; supplier bank-editor restriction/snapshot same as payment. `PAYMENT.REVERSE` one person, unused only. |
| Supplier refund received | `PAYMENT.READ` reads; `PAYMENT.REFUND` creates/edits/deletes/posts DRAFT; no SUBMITTED/waiting flow. | SingleStep; `PAYMENT.REVERSE` reverses. |
| Fiscal registration/retry | `FISCAL.MANAGE`; registration change reason; document read rights still apply. | Separate configuration audit; no money approval implied by retry. |

Permission names above have the `ACCOUNTS.` prefix. Reuse existing codes; no automatic user-service migration. Contributors include everyone who created, edited or submitted, even before rejection. Resolve Company Owner freshly from the standard-role membership through CompanyOwnerLookup; custom all-powerful roles and platform sessions do not qualify. An Owner contributor needs explicit `ownerSelfApproval`; record it on source, journal, audit and event. Direct approval from DRAFT is only for that Owner's contributed draft. Do not extend the money-document exception to unrelated bank-change approval safety or silently waive the second person on write-off recovery (Q5).

Snapshot refund payee bank details at submit, compare/re-take under approval and freeze; show an approval warning for changes, never an unreviewed switch. Mask data in ordinary responses; full reveal only through audited access. A pending first customer bank request blocks BANK-account refund approval (`BANK_DETAILS_PENDING`); cash refund follows the contract. Missing bank route warnings remain distinct from pending-request refusals. Carry the existing supplier bank-change policy into advance payments, including fail-closed authorization lookup.

Refusals are atomic: no header/counters/allocation/journal/number/audit/domain event/outbox is left by a rejected money action. Audit permitted reveal/security attempts separately from financial success. Required cases:

- Tenant/resource ownership and effective endpoint permission first; cross-company/party IDs do not disclose records. Module disabled, books not live, before go-live, period closed/13, future date, inactive/header/unmapped account, invalid branch/dimension pass existing errors through.
- `VERSION_CONFLICT`; wrong lifecycle; `TWO_PERSON_RULE` (including BANK_DETAILS_EDITOR); `OWNER_CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED`; same document approved by another actor → `ALREADY_APPROVED`.
- `ADVANCE_LINES_NOT_EQUAL`, `ADVANCE_FIELDS_NOT_ALLOWED`, invalid SALES/PURCHASES tax side/kind/date; NO/IS customer advance with anything but OUT_OF_SCOPE → `TAX_CODE_INVALID`/`NO_TAX_ON_ADVANCES`; zero/negative or >20 advance lines invalid.
- `ADVANCE_EXCEEDS_OPEN` for deductions/consumption; `ADVANCE_IN_USE` for reversal while consumed. Duplicate advance source IDs, wrong party, unposted/reversed advance and advance dated after target refused.
- `REFUND_NOT_FULLY_ALLOCATED`; exhausted/unavailable sources; duplicates; wrong customer/supplier, currency or debit/credit direction. Do not permit advances as ordinary allocation sources.
- `WRITE_OFF_EXCEEDS_OPEN`; missing/short/long reason (6–500); invalid relief/tax evidence; no invented relief for opening items with no stored tax. Typed opening tax follows B C-44 or no relief.
- Duplicate supplier prepayment invoice follows C-4 normalization/existing bill/advance evidence checks; input VAT without prepayment evidence refused or recorded as ordinary on-account payment without tax, explicitly chosen by user (Q4).
- `BANK_LINE_MATCHED` on reversal; `FISCAL_REGISTRATION_OVERLAP`, `FISCAL_STATE`, `FISCAL_NOT_ACCEPTED`, `FISCAL_VALIDATION_FAILED`; `LEGAL_CONTENT_MISSING` as applicable. New error codes/shapes require contract alignment and app wording, not generic success/failure fallbacks.

Create operations retain an optional Idempotency-Key per user attempt and canonical body hash; same key/body returns the original draft, changed body → `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED`. Do not clear keys on timeout. Post/reverse locks serialize in-process keys: `ADVANCE_RECEIPT:{id}:1`, `CUSTOMER_REFUND:{id}:1`, `AR_WRITE_OFF:{id}:1`, `SUPPLIER_REFUND:{id}:1`; supplier advance source/key convention is Q3. Reversals append `:reverse`. Ordinary invoice/bill keys remain unchanged. Same actor already completed → stored 200; another actor → existing already-approved/reversed conflict. Replay precedes stale-version checks where the existing lifecycle requires it; no duplicate timeline/audit/event/number. Canonical amount equality ignores formatting, not meaning. No catch-and-reread or SAVEPOINT inside the shared posting transaction; unique index violations are defects. The app rereads after uncertain money results before retrying.

## 5. Lock order and V43 compatibility

Perform remote Org/authz/provider validation and immutable fact fetches before acquiring database row locks. Transaction is READ COMMITTED, one in-process engine call and one commit; validations rerun under the locks. Discover IDs with unlocked reads, then verify the locked snapshot still names exactly those sources; changed membership/version aborts and requires reload, never adds a late source lock.

1. Setup row in final mode: first posting FOR UPDATE, subsequent FOR SHARE; never upgrade.
2. **Sources before targets.** Lock every source header in stable ID order (stable table/type tie-breaker if needed), then the required advance child lines in stable order. Lock target invoice/bill/refund headers in ascending ID order. Party row FOR SHARE **last among subledger rows**, after every source/target lock. Then engine period → accounts ascending ID → journal sequence → document sequence via documentRefProvider → balance keys sorted. No external calls under locks.
3. Customer/supplier refund: credit/debit sources and advance parents/lines precede the refund target. Apply this even to refund draft edit/delete/submit and reversals that also touch sources; do not inherit the older B §7.16 prose that locks refund first. Approval cannot lock refund then chase sources while allocation does the reverse.
4. Final invoice/bill deduction: advance source parents/lines before invoice/bill target; credit-note/debit-note paths continue source credit document → original invoice/bill target. Write-off → invoice target. Bulk targets always sorted. Supplier/customer last includes bank snapshot checks.
5. Refund reversal uses that same source-before-refund order, then engine original-journal lock/reversal. A receipt/payment reversal locks its source before the active allocation targets in ID order; target queries cannot also lock debit-note/credit-note sources as targets. Undo follows the identical graph. Locking tests must include these mixed paths, not only isolated posts.

V43 requirements are acceptance criteria, not permission to relax guards:

- `posted_xid` is stamped by DB; only same-posting-transaction inserts of period-bearing lines can attach to a posted journal. Existing posted headers keep historical NULL. No GUC bypass or retroactive line mutation, including fiscal metadata work.
- Posted header changes only the valid same-company posted RV backlink. Reversal adds RV lines; it never edits original lines/tax tags. Every posted line has a period at deferred constraint check.
- AR/AP line and line-tax guards lock/check both parents, blocking moves into/out of posted/reversed parents. Resolve child accounts/tax snapshots before flipping source headers POSTED; posted receipt/refund updated_by/updated_at are not writable.
- Credited counters only increase on posted invoice/bill/opening originals. Advance availability is a distinct counter model; do not misuse credited counters for restoration.
- Legal sequences start at 1, advance exactly +1, never delete/reset/compensate. Rollback restores the increment automatically.
- Preserve posted receipt/refund/advance child immutability. AP restoration needs a narrow forward migration with immutable consumption/reversal evidence (Q2), never disabling all triggers or allowing arbitrary counter decreases.
