Date: 2026-10-31. Static source audit; no runtime exploitation or Gradle execution.

Additional exposure depends on a signed employee_id claim or on enabling payroll under local/test profiles. No ordinary-user cross-company exploit was confirmed in the reviewed accounts, user, org, audit, or gateway flows. This is a source-review result, not a guarantee about deployed configuration.

### TI-02 — HIGH — Foreign HR references bridge companies and contaminate manager/team queries

**Failure scenario:** An A HR writer creates or transfers/promotes an A employee while supplying B's manager employee ID. The primary employee gate passes for A, but the new manager is accepted without a company comparison. A B caller authorized for that manager can then retrieve A employee/team data because directReports filters only managerEmployeeId. Foreign department/designation/branch references and lifecycle targets can likewise be persisted without checking their company. History writes also accept unrelated department/designation/actor references. The manager-read scenario requires the B-side manager/read permission and the poisoned relationship; this is not a standalone arbitrary B employee read by the A writer.

### TI-03 — HIGH, conditional — Omitted HR filters authorize one company and query every company

### TI-04 — HIGH in local/test; production blocked — Payroll data remains globally shared

## Policy question and non-findings

- Historical inbox access after leaving a company: notification/workflow lists are bound to recipient/participant, not current membership. A globally active former A member can still read A notifications/workflow summaries previously addressed to them and mark their own notification read. The code explicitly uses a personal-inbox policy, so this is not classified as a confirmed vulnerability. If company revocation must remove historical inbox access, require an authorized-company set and stored company check on both list and id operations. Evidence: [NotificationController.kt:78](../../../NeoERP-BackEnd/services/audit-service/src/main/kotlin/com/neovatus/audit/delivery/http/controller/NotificationController.kt#L78); [NotificationService.kt:65](../../../NeoERP-BackEnd/services/audit-service/src/main/kotlin/com/neovatus/audit/application/service/NotificationService.kt#L65); [WorkflowController.kt:73](../../../NeoERP-BackEnd/services/audit-service/src/main/kotlin/com/neovatus/audit/delivery/http/controller/WorkflowController.kt#L73); [WorkflowRepositoryAdapter.kt:53](../../../NeoERP-BackEnd/services/audit-service/src/main/kotlin/com/neovatus/audit/infrastructure/persistence/adapter/WorkflowRepositoryAdapter.kt#L53).
- Request-supplied company IDs are not automatically bugs. User/org/accounts person routes authorize the selected company using token principal, memberships or platform session. Trusted machine routes deliberately accept company from their producing service; no person token was found able to become that machine merely by supplying a company ID.
- Platform staff and ROLE_SUPER_ADMIN are intentionally capable of cross-company operations under explicit permissions/reachable-company scopes. Backend AGENTS.md requires per-request IAM lifecycle/DB resolution; broad OAuth scopes or token realm-role claims alone are not sufficient. No ordinary tenant-to-platform elevation was established.
- Accounts system queues, ledger checks, backfill and cleanup intentionally scan companies. Ordinary person controllers do not expose their raw selectors. SQL without company in a genuinely platform reference table or worker claim is not itself a tenant leak.
- No companyless tenant-result cache, cross-company accounts replay collision, or accounts numbering collision was confirmed. HR/payroll machine-token caches are service credentials. Global payroll codes and shift codes are recorded in TI-04/TI-05.
- No application/backend source was changed and none of the proposed architecture tests was implemented or run. Severity and paths are source-based; deployment profiles, external issuer claim configuration and live permissions were not inspected.
