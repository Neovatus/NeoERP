Checked and found correct (no row):

- `BigDecimal` everywhere, with no floats on money.
- `company_id` on every company query.
- Pack test coverage for the six countries.
- ORG-FACTS-2 legal-name and company-code consumption (fails closed).

`StatementPerformanceTest` and the A3 acceptance and load scripts were moved to CP-A3.3 by ruling ACC-B-7, so they are not counted as missing.

**Verdict.** Year-end close, reopen and lock, closing-journal exclusion and the statement figures are sound, and the DB guards cannot be bypassed.
One money-facing defect must be fixed before Phase 2 is signed off: P2S-1, a false trial-balance difference on the year-end balance sheet. P2S-2 should also be fixed: every balance sheet is still labelled "management".
The rest are contract-alignment items: DK layout, two target sources, tautological checks, go-live comparatives, preview on CREATED books, trigger coupling, and the unmapped target. They can be scheduled into CP-A3.3.
