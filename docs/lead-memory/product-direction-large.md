Jabran 2026-11-03: "we gonna build for large customer… our vision is clear from day first large scale with modules based, what customer want". A showcase that already meets large-customer expectations is what gets pitched.

**Why:** without a large-customer showcase there is nothing to pitch; retrofitting dimensions/budgets onto posted documents later means migrating live data.

**How to apply:** never plan large-customer features (dimensions ADR-021, budgets ADR-022, reporting ADR-023, approval limits, consolidation) as a "release 3" afterthought; flag any new money-line table that lacks the dimension set. Features stay optional per company and off by default. Recorded in docs/large-customer-roadmap.md and NeoERP-App/docs/module-sequence.md. Related: [[release-gates]].
