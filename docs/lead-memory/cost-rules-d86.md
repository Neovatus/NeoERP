Mechanical fixes and fully decided mechanical flows go to Sonnet (a fresh Sonnet builder with a short fix brief is fine); Opus only for discovery, money, security, design-heavy work. No session or agent (leads, flow leads, builders, testers) grows past ~50 % context: compact with a keep line or hand off to a new agent/session.

**Why:** Jabran 2026-11-25 at 40 % of the weekly limit; resumed Opus builders grew to 850–920k tokens and mechanical JSON flows cost ~400k on Opus.

**How to apply:** pick the model per flow/fix in each brief; tell flow leads; watch context and hand off before 50 %. Amends [[workflow-v2]] "same builder" for mechanical fixes; see [[save-usage-limits]].
