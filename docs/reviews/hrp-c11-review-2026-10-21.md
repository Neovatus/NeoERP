## 1. C10 fixes: PASS

- **C10-1:** the parent must be the childless lineage head. Otherwise the request is refused with LINEAGE_HEAD_CHANGED and `details.headVersionId`, and nothing is written. Code: `payroll-data.jsx:3089`. This matches P4 §2b.4.
- **C10-2:** `cancelOnly` must be a boolean and is only valid with CANCEL. After transmission, `payCFilingStatus` derives CANCELLED. A further correction on a cancelled-only head is refused. Code: `payroll-data.jsx:3089-3090` and `payCFilingStatus`.
- **C10-3:** an empty manual reference returns `VALIDATION` with field `reference`. Code: `payUpload`.
- **C10-4:** the DK cutoff applies only to A-skat/AM, whether the field is declared or the frozen withholding/AM amount changed. Code: `payroll-data.jsx:3105-3106`.

## 2. Alignment security: PASS

- **Reason gates:** the policy row now saves through a reason gate, and `payPolicySet` refuses a reason under 6 characters. The publish switch keeps the stored value until the gate saves, and the off action uses the danger variant. A read-only viewer sees status pills.
- **Earlier fixes, no regression:**
  - CA-2: the payee reveal now masks the current account.
  - CA-14: there is no national-id change request; NID changes only through SENSITIVE.UPDATE, with mapped human messages.

Low notes:

## 3. Posting preview: FAIL

The following hold:

- The preview writes nothing: `readyToPost:false`, `readOnly`, `coverageOnly`, and there are no amounts, journal, payment or deposit writers.
- Accounts come only from the existing `PAY_ACCOUNTS` via `payRoleAccount`, and the fixtures omit account numbers.
- Every 2026.4 role reads "not yet mapped (pack 2026.4)": HOLIDAY_FUND, UNION_DUES, the SLP pair, and the FI Työllisyysrahasto component.
- The P1-39/P3-28 approved-account and maker bars are kept: a waiting first account is excluded, the predecessor is masked, and maker-blocked is shown.
- The P3-34/P4-14 payment–deposit–filing links are read-only.
- These roles match the rules: IS PENSION_FUND_PAYABLE per fund, IS tryggingagjald to Skatturinn, FI Employment Fund to EMPLOYER_CONTRIB_PAYABLE with AUTHORITY:FI.TYOLLISYYSRAHASTO, DK FUND to HOLIDAY_FUND_PAYABLE with AUTHORITY:DK.FERIEKONTO, and UNION_DUES_PAYABLE in every country.

Findings:

- **D-1 (Medium).** `payroll-data.jsx:3212`: the FI employee unemployment (TVR) row stays on UNEMPLOYMENT_INSURANCE_PAYABLE with no payee. This contradicts `payroll-v2-p8.md:264`, which says a NONE-tag role that drops `payeeRef` is not a fallback. P2 §5.6 still carries NONE-tag TVR rows at `payroll-v2-p2.md:357,359,370`, and these conflict with row 358 (ACC-237). **Fix:**
  - Contract owner: align P2 §5.6 rows 357, 359 and 370 to the ACC-237 Employment Fund mapping.
  - Rig: give the employee share the payee AUTHORITY:FI.TYOLLISYYSRAHASTO and mark it "not yet mapped".
- **D-2 (Medium).** `payroll-v2-p7.md:211`: the DK FUND row says "Release the source holiday liability". P2 §5.7 (`payroll-v2-p2.md:392`) says DK hourly feriepenge are not an accrual: they are a taxable DR HOLIDAY_PAY_EXPENSE earning plus a net CR HOLIDAY_FUND_PAYABLE deduction. **Fix:** reword P7:211 to the P2 §5.7 two-part shape.
- **D-3 (Low).** DK coverage gaps:
  - P7:208 requires a Samlet Betaling employer-schemes row (EMPLOYER_CONTRIB_*), but the rig has none for DK (`payroll-data.jsx:3207-3219`).
  - FUND mode omits the DR HOLIDAY_PAY_EXPENSE earning part (`payroll-data.jsx:3245`).
  - ATP shows an account as mapped, but P7 says an unsupported Samlet tag must block.

  **Fix:** add these rows, and mark ATP as pending until pack 2026.4.
- **D-4 (Low).** `payroll-data.jsx:3227,3243`: the payee tags `PENSION_FUND:<payee id>` and `UNION:<country>:<id>` are invented formats. P2 uses `PENSION_FUND:{kennitala}` and `UNION:{registry number}`. **Fix:** show the registry/kennitala tag, or "not supplied".
- **D-5 (Low).** `payroll-data.jsx:3229`: SE gets an employee pension deduction row, but `payroll-v2-p6.md:171` says the pension is employer only. **Fix:** omit the row for SE, or mark it with the applicability-pending note.

## 4. Permissions and privacy: FAIL

The following hold:

- `payDPreview` and `payDRunOptions` check PAYROLL.RUN.VIEW, company scope and the Neovatus grant.
- Posted-only viewers cannot list or open unposted runs, and the synthetic sample is hidden from them.
- The persona key resets the selection.

Findings:

- **P-1 (Medium).** `payroll-data.jsx:3220,3242`: the union and pension-provider names come from every employee in the run, including confidential employees, and there is no PAYROLL.CONFIDENTIAL.READ check. Union membership is special-category data, and a small union can be traced back to a person. **Fix:** leave out confidential employees' records unless the viewer holds CONFIDENTIAL.READ, or show a payee count only.
- **P-2 (Low).** `payroll-setup.jsx:605,914`: a Setup viewer without RUN.VIEW sees an "unavailable" evidence card. Under ux §6 it should not be shown at all. **Fix:** render nothing when `payDRunOptions` is empty or the preview is denied.
